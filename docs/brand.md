# Zebra

Name: Zebra. Descriptor: HDR to SDR for Mac. Tagline: Wild color. Tamed.

The zebra's restrained cinematic color flows into charcoal and ivory, a visual metaphor for
HDR-to-SDR conversion. SDR video remains full color; this is not a grayscale tool.
The native window stays quiet and simple. The icon carries the personality.

The app was previously called Daylight. Its bundle identifier remains
`io.github.barryanders.daylight` so macOS treats the renamed app as the same app.

## Artwork provenance

`Assets/zebra-icon.png` was created using the built-in image-generation tool.
The build script scales the original transparent PNG into an ICNS; no online
service is involved when using the app.

Selected artwork: a dimensional, cinematic zebra kicking up,
with ivory/obsidian surfaces, a turned expressive head and restrained teal/amber
light flowing along its back. The user rejected candidates 1–4: loud rainbow,
portrait, bulky overhead animal, then flat graphic silhouette.

Saved image: `../Assets/zebra-icon.png`. Built-in image generation was used.

Prompt: An artistically stunning macOS icon for Zebra. A sculptural,
cinematic zebra in a dramatic bucking pose viewed from high above at a three-quarter
angle, athletic narrow torso, graceful long legs, arched slender neck, lifted
hindquarters, exactly four legs, one flowing tail. Small head turns toward viewer
with a sharp confident expression. Contemporary art: carved ivory and polished
obsidian, flowing stripes around dimensional form, satin surfaces and controlled
specular highlights, dramatic studio lighting. Not flat vector, cartoon mascot,
cute toy, realistic furry animal, fat or bulky. No oversized head or muzzle.
Visually hot, stylish, editorial, expressive. Restrained cinematic color along
back and stripe edges: petrol teal shadows through luminous honey into ember-copper,
like exposed color film. Predominantly black/ivory, no rainbow bands or colored
mane. Elegant legible silhouette. Full animal contained with negative space in a
near-black rounded square, transparent exterior. No props, floor, sparkles, neon
trails, clutter or text. One square finished app icon.
